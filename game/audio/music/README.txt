Music for Wisp Forge (made with Suno). Name a track by when it plays (.mp3 or .ogg):
  surface_day*, surface_night*, underground*, deep*, boss*, space*, title*
A track for one kind of planet starts with the kind, then the moment:
  volcanic_surface_day.mp3, frozen_surface_night.mp3, ocean_underground_2.mp3 ...
  kinds: temperate, volcanic, ocean, desert, marsh, frozen, wild, realm
A planet with no track of its own for that moment plays the common one.
